**Title:** Real Madrid 5-1 PSV: 1-0 down at half-time then Bellingham changed it #Shorts

**Description:**
Real Madrid were 1-0 down to PSV at the Bernabeu after Ivan Perisic's 32nd-minute penalty. Jude Bellingham came on at half-time. Federico Valverde levelled, Bellingham volleyed in off the post and Kylian Mbappe (2) and Vinicius Junior finished it 5-1.

It is Real Madrid's 10th straight win in all competitions. They have 15 points from five Champions League league-phase games and lead PSG by three.

Elsewhere Arsenal drew 1-1 with Dortmund and Manchester City needed Rayan Cherki's 86th-minute goal to draw 2-2 with Napoli. Arsenal host Real Madrid in two weeks.
