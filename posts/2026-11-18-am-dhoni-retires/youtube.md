**Title**
MS Dhoni retires from the IPL: CSK make him team mentor for IPL 2027 #Shorts

**Description**
MS Dhoni has retired from the IPL. He told Chennai Super Kings on the IPL 2027 retention deadline day and will stay with the franchise as team mentor from the 2027 season. He led CSK to five IPL titles.

Why now: a calf injury kept him out of the whole IPL 2026 season and there will be no farewell match at Chepauk. He stays in team management and is reported to keep a big say in selection.

What's next: CSK go to the December 16 mini-auction in Goa with Rs 28.05 crore and 6 squad places to fill, including 2 overseas slots. Lucknow Super Giants have the biggest purse at Rs 47.45 crore.
