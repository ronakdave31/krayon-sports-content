**Title:** India vs Japan 4-0: India women climb to World No. 4 in hockey | Asian Champions Trophy #Shorts

**Description:**
India beat Japan 4-0 in Bhopal to finish the Women's Asian Champions Trophy group stage with five wins from five. Lalremsiami, Deepika and Neha Goyal made it 3-0 by half-time and Deepika added a fourth in the 48th minute.

The win lifts India to No. 4 in the FIH world rankings, their highest ever. They reached No. 5 for the first time only a day earlier after beating China 6-2. The previous best was No. 6 in 2022.

India scored 22 goals and conceded 5 in the group. The defending champions play their semi-final on Friday, against Malaysia as reported by RevSportz.
