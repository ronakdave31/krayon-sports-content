**Title:** Gukesh vs Sindarov Game 3: Three games, three draws at the World Chess Championship #Shorts

**Description:**
D Gukesh held Javokhir Sindarov to a 60-move draw with the black pieces in Game 3 of the World Chess Championship in Geneva. After three draws the match is level at 1.5-1.5.

Gukesh was ready with the Berlin Defence. Sindarov used a lot of his clock in the opening. Engines gave White an edge at times but Gukesh kept finding the moves and was ahead on the clock in the endgame. Players cannot agree a draw before move 40, which is why this one ran to 60 moves.

Friday is the first rest day. Gukesh has White in Game 4. 11 of the 14 games remain and the first to 7.5 points is world champion.
