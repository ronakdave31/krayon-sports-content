**Title:** India vs Pakistan SAFF Championship 2026 final: India's 10th title or Pakistan's first? #Shorts

**Description:**
India and Pakistan meet in the 2026 SAFF Championship final in Dhaka. Pakistan are in their first SAFF final ever after a late 1-0 win over hosts Bangladesh. India are the defending champions with nine titles.

The twist: India's senior team is on tour in New Zealand, so coach Naushad Moosa's reserve side is defending the title. It beat Bhutan 2-1 in the semi-final.

The warning sign is the group game on Nov 5. India led 3-0 after 31 minutes and Pakistan scored twice in stoppage time to make it 3-2.
