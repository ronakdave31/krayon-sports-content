**Title**
Vaishali beats Arjun Erigaisi in Total Chess Budapest: last place knocks out the leader #Shorts

**Description**
Arjun Erigaisi led Group D of the Total Chess event in Budapest going into the last game. After Vachier-Lagrave won, Arjun needed a win to top the group. R Vaishali, last in the group, beat him instead and Arjun resigned on move 61.

Arjun finished on 16 points, level with Hans Niemann but third on tie-break. Only group winners reach the four-player title knockout, so no Indian is left in the title race. Praggnanandhaa finished last in Group B and Magnus Carlsen went winless in the group stage.

The title semi-finals are MVL vs Giri and Firouzja vs Caruana on Nov 17-18. Arjun plays Jorden van Foreest in the 9th to 12th place bracket and the finals are on Nov 19-20.
