**Title**
Bengaluru FC 2-0 Mumbai City: fewer shots but top of the ISL #Shorts

**Description**
Bengaluru FC beat Mumbai City 2-0 at the Sree Kanteerava Stadium to go top of the ISL 2026-27 table with 13 points from 5 games. Sunil Chhetri captained the side in his 200th ISL appearance.

Mumbai had 51% of the ball and 16 shots to Bengaluru's 9. It was 0-0 at half-time before Borja Martinez scored a 57th-minute penalty and substitute Ryan Williams made it 2-0 in the 85th minute.

Bengaluru are one point clear of Mohun Bagan. NorthEast United can go above them with a win over FC Goa on Saturday, while Mumbai are still without a win in 5 games.
