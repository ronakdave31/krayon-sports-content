**Title**
KL Rahul 124* vs New Zealand: First Indian Opener With Tons In All 4 SENA Countries #Shorts

**Description**
KL Rahul made an unbeaten 124 off 265 balls on day 3 of the first Test in Wellington. It makes him the first Indian opener with Test hundreds in Australia, England, South Africa and New Zealand.

India are on top because Rahul was in both of the first two stands: 130 with Yashasvi Jaiswal and 165 with Devdutt Padikkal. India have had 100 stands for each of the first two wickets in a SENA Test only four times.

At tea on day 3 India were 344 for 2 in reply to New Zealand's 368, with Rahul and Shubman Gill at the crease.
