**Title:** India's new sports league panel: IPL, Reliance, JioStar on 14-member committee #Shorts

**Description:**
The Sports Ministry has formed a 14-member committee to build a framework for professionally managed and financially sustainable sports leagues in India. It has eight weeks to report.

The panel includes BCCI CEO and IPL COO Hemang Amin, IOA CEO Raghuram Iyer, Reliance, GMR, Adani and JSW, plus broadcasters JioStar, Sony, DD Sports and Zee. Eight of the 14 seats belong to corporates and broadcasters. PTI reports the revived hockey league already faces the prospect of a shutdown within two years.

The committee will pick priority sports (Olympic, women's, youth, indigenous and emerging), suggest a model for each sport and look at sponsorship, media rights, CSR money, viability gap funding and cheaper access to government venues.
