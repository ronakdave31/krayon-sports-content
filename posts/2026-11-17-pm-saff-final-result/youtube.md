**Title**
India beat Pakistan 2-1 in SAFF Championship final: record 10th title #Shorts

**Description**
India came from 1-0 down to beat Pakistan 2-1 in the SAFF Championship final at the National Stadium in Dhaka. Alamgir Ghazi put Pakistan ahead in first-half stoppage time.

The final turned in the 59th minute. Pakistan's Junaid Shah was sent off after a VAR review and one minute later Suhail Ahmad Bhat equalised. Sandip Mandi headed in Abdul Rabeeh's corner in the 88th minute to win it.

It is India's record 10th SAFF title and the first time they have won three editions in a row. Pakistan were playing their first SAFF final.
