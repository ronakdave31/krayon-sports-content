**Title:** Spain 2-3 England: Nations League win ends Spain's 43-game unbeaten run #Shorts

**Description:**
England beat Spain 3-2 in Madrid in the Nations League. Harry Kane scored a penalty before half-time, Ezri Konsa headed in a Declan Rice corner and Ben White made it 3-0 by the 57th minute.

Spain had not lost in 43 games, a run that included a Euro and a World Cup title. After Elliot Anderson's red card Spain pulled two back through Oyarzabal and Nico Williams, but James Trafford's stoppage-time double save kept England in front.

England top Group A3 on goal difference. Both teams go into the quarter-finals in March 2027 and the draw is this Friday.
