**Title**
Las Vegas GP 2026 practice: Verstappen 13th as rain wipes out FP1 #Shorts

**Description**
Rain washed out the first practice session at the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix with not a single lap time set. In a damp FP2 George Russell went fastest in 1:47.272, just 0.071s ahead of Pierre Gasly with Charles Leclerc third.

Max Verstappen was 13th, 2.869s back. He reported a lack of power and a red flag for plastic on the track kept him in the garage, so his only timed lap came in the final minutes.

It matters because Verstappen leads Russell by 17 points in the fight for P2 behind champion Kimi Antonelli. Can he bounce back in qualifying?
