**Title:** Tanvi Sharma beats Unnati Hooda from a game down to reach Syed Modi International final #Shorts

**Description:**
Tanvi Sharma, 17, came back to beat fellow Indian Unnati Hooda 11-21, 21-10, 21-13 in the Syed Modi International women's singles semi-finals in Lucknow.

After losing the first game she let Unnati score only 23 points in the next two. A year ago Tanvi went out in the semi-finals of this event. In August she won the Taipei Open Super 300.

Sunday's final is against Thailand's Supanida Katethong. Tanvi is the second seed and the winner takes US$18,750.
