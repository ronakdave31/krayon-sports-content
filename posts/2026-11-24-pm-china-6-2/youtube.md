**Title:** India 6-2 China: Deepika hat-trick in 14 minutes | Women's Asian Champions Trophy hockey #Shorts

**Description:**
India beat China 6-2 at the Women's Asian Champions Trophy in Bhopal on Tuesday. Deepika scored three goals by the 14th minute: a field goal in the 1st and penalty corners in the 6th and 14th. Lalremsiami, Neha Goyal and Sakshi Rana scored the others.

China are the Paris Olympics silver medallists but RevSportz reports 13 of their players made international debuts here and head coach Alyson Annan was not with the team. It is still India's fourth win in four games.

India and Japan are both on 12 points. India lead on goal difference (+15 to +8) and the two meet on Wednesday at 7 pm IST for top spot before the semi-finals.
