**Description**
Arsenal drew 2-2 at Newcastle on Saturday. Eberechi Eze put them ahead, Harvey Barnes and Malick Thiaw turned it around for Newcastle and Noni Madueke equalised in the 78th minute. Goalkeeper David Raya went off with a shoulder injury.

Why it matters: Manchester City also drew, 1-1 at home to Fulham. Rodrigo Muniz scored for Fulham, Elliot Anderson levelled and a Haaland goal was ruled out. City had 15 shots but only 3 on target.

What's next: Arsenal are on 28 points, six clear of Brighton and City. Brighton play Hull on Sunday and can cut the gap to three.
