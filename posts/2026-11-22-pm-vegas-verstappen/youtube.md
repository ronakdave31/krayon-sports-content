**Title**
Verstappen wins chaotic Las Vegas GP by 0.639s as Piastri goes 17th to 3rd #Shorts

**Description**
Max Verstappen won the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole, beating Lando Norris by 0.639s after a race where the lead changed hands again and again. Oscar Piastri started 17th after a 10-place power unit penalty and finished third. Kimi Antonelli went from sixth to fourth.

Why it matters: George Russell started on the front row but locked up and hit the barrier late on while trying to pass Antonelli for second. He finished ninth and dropped to fourth in the standings.

What's next: two races left. Verstappen has 304 points in the fight for P2, ahead of Lewis Hamilton on 267 and Russell and Norris on 264.
