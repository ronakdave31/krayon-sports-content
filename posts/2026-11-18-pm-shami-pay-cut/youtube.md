**Title:** Mohammed Shami traded to Rajasthan Royals with a pay cut: LSG to RR for Rs 8 crore #Shorts

**Description:**
Lucknow Super Giants have traded Mohammed Shami and M Siddharth to Rajasthan Royals in a three-player deal. Sam Curran moves from RR to LSG at his existing fee of Rs 2.4 crore.

Shami joins RR at a revised fee of Rs 8 crore, Rs 2 crore less than LSG paid for him. In IPL 2026 he took 12 wickets in 13 matches at 9.00 runs an over. He is 36 and has retired from Tests but still has 145 wickets in 132 IPL matches.

At RR he joins Jofra Archer and Nandre Burger in the pace attack. Siddharth moves at his existing Rs 75 lakh and the IPL 2027 mini-auction is on December 16.
