**Title:** Thomas Cup 2028 in New Delhi: 24 teams and best-of-three ties explained #Shorts

**Description:**
BWF confirmed on Wednesday that New Delhi will host the 2028 Thomas & Uber Cup Finals. It is the first time the Finals come to India since 2014.

The format is changing. The field grows from 16 teams to 24, in four groups of six, and each team plays four ties before the top 12 reach the knockouts. Ties become best of three instead of best of five. Every match won earns 1 point, with 2 bonus points for winning the tie.

India won their first Thomas Cup in 2022 in Bangkok and took bronze in 2026 in Horsens, Denmark. In 2028 they play the new format in front of a home crowd.
