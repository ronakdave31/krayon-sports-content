**Description**
India scored 327 runs for three wickets on day 3 of the first Test in Wellington. They started the day 209 behind and finished it 486 for 4, a lead of 118 over New Zealand's 368.

All of India's top five made fifties: KL Rahul 136, Shubman Gill 98, Yashasvi Jaiswal 85, Devdutt Padikkal 80 and Rishabh Pant 55 not out. Rahul survived an lbw appeal on 5 that New Zealand did not review. Jacob Duffy finished with 1 for 180 from 29 overs.

Two days are left. India lead by 118 but still have to bowl New Zealand out a second time to win.
