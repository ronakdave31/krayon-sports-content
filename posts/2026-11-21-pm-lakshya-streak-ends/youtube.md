**Title:** Lakshya Sen's 13-match winning streak ends vs Antonsen | Hong Kong Open 2026 semi-final #Shorts

**Description:**
Lakshya Sen reached the Hong Kong Open 2026 semi-finals on a 13-match winning streak after back-to-back titles at the Hylo Open and the Kumamoto Masters. Anders Antonsen ended it on Saturday with a reported 21-13, 21-13 win in 42 minutes. Satwik-Chirag also lost their semi-final 18-21, 19-21 to Man Wei Chong and Tee Kai Wun.

The streak equalled Kidambi Srikanth's 13 straight wins from 2017. Antonsen now leads the head-to-head 6-3 and has won every meeting between the two in Asia.

Next up: Lakshya and Antonsen are both in the BWF World Tour Finals in Hangzhou from December 9 to 13.
