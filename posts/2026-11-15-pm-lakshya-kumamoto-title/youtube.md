**Title**
Lakshya Sen wins Kumamoto Masters Japan after losing game 1 vs Koki Watanabe #Shorts

**Description**
Lakshya Sen lost the first game 13-21 to home favourite Koki Watanabe in the Kumamoto Masters Japan final. He came back to win 21-18, 21-14 in 64 minutes and lift the Super 500 title.

It is his second BWF World Tour title in two weeks after he won the Hylo Open in Germany on Nov 1. The turning point was a tight second game. In the decider he led 11-7 at the break.

Next is the race to the BWF World Tour Finals in Hangzhou, where only the top 8 qualify. Lakshya was 12th before this week and reports now place him 6th.
