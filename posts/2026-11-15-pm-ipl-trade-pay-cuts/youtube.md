**Title**
IPL 2027 trades: Rishabh Pant takes Rs 12 crore pay cut, Hardik Pandya gets a raise #Shorts

**Description**
Rishabh Pant is back at Delhi Capitals for IPL 2027 at a revised Rs 15 crore, down from the record Rs 27 crore Lucknow paid before IPL 2025. Kuldeep Yadav moves to Lucknow at his existing Rs 13.5 crore.

Pant is not alone. Liam Livingstone joins CSK at Rs 6 crore (down from Rs 13 crore) and T Natarajan returns to SRH at Rs 5 crore (down from Rs 10.75 crore). The big raise belongs to Hardik Pandya, who joins Punjab Kings at Rs 18 crore in the swap for Arshdeep Singh.

Lower fees leave teams more purse for the mini-auction in Goa on December 16. Retention lists are reportedly due November 17.
