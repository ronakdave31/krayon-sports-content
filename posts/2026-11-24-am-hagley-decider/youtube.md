**Title**
India vs New Zealand 2nd Test: India's only Test at Hagley Oval lasted three days #Shorts

**Description**
India have played one Test at Hagley Oval in Christchurch. In 2020 they led by seven runs on first innings and were then bowled out for 124 in 46 overs. Trent Boult took 4/28 and Tim Southee 3/36 as New Zealand won by seven wickets inside three days.

Now the ground hosts the decider. The Wellington Test was drawn so the two-Test series is level. A win takes the series for India and a loss hands it to New Zealand.

The second Test starts on Friday at 3:30 am IST. A draw leaves the series level.
