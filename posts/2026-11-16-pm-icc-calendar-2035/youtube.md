**Title**
India to host 2029 Champions Trophy and 2031 World Cup: ICC calendar to 2035 explained #Shorts

**Description**
The ICC Board met in Bali on November 15 and fixed the windows for every men's event until 2035. India host the 2029 Champions Trophy from October 9 to 28, their first at home since 2006. India and Bangladesh co-host the 2031 World Cup from October 9 to November 23.

The T20 World Cup now alternates between an October to November window (Australia and New Zealand in 2028) and a July to August window (England, Ireland and Scotland in 2030). The WTC Final stays in June every two years, with England hosting in 2029 and 2031.

Six of the 12 events from 2028 to 2035 still have no confirmed host, so more bids are coming.
