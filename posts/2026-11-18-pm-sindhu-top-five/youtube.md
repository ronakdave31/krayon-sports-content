**Title**
PV Sindhu back in the BWF world top 5 for the first time since 2022 #Shorts

**Description**
PV Sindhu is world No. 5 in the BWF women's singles rankings released on November 17. It is the first time she has been in the top five since November 2022. She was 11th in early October.

Why now: in July she ended a 19-month title drought at the Japan Open, a Super 750 event, beating four-time winner Akane Yamaguchi 21-17, 21-17 in the final. She then finished runner-up at the French Open and won the Hylo Open on November 1.

What's next: the 31-year-old has qualified for the BWF World Tour Finals in Hangzhou from December 9 to 13.
