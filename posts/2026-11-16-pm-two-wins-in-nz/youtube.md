**Title**
NZ vs India Test series: India have won only 2 Test series in New Zealand | Gill, Bumrah #Shorts

**Description**
India won the ODI series in New Zealand 3-1. Now come two Tests in the World Test Championship: the first at the Basin Reserve in Wellington from November 19 (3:30 am IST) and the second at Hagley Oval in Christchurch from November 27.

History is against India. They have won only two Test series in New Zealand: 3-1 under Pataudi in 1968 and 1-0 under Dhoni in 2009, when they won in Hamilton by 10 wickets. New Zealand won the next two, 1-0 in 2014 and 2-0 in 2020. In 2024 New Zealand also became the first team to whitewash India 3-0 in a three-match home Test series.

This time Shubman Gill leads with KL Rahul as vice-captain. Jasprit Bumrah is back in the Test squad and Rishabh Pant is named subject to fitness.
