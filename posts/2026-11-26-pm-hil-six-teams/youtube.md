**Title**
Hockey India League 2026/27: Six Men's Teams, Rs 4 Crore Cap, Auction Dec 7 #Shorts

**Description**
The Hockey India League returns in the first week of January 2027. Hockey India confirmed six men's teams and four women's teams for the 2026/27 season. Last season the men's league had eight.

The player auction is on December 7 in New Delhi. Men's teams can spend up to Rs 4 crore each and women's teams Rs 2 crore. Base prices are Rs 2 lakh, Rs 5 lakh or Rs 10 lakh. Players from the Ranchi Royals men's team and the Governing Council team go into the auction.

Next up: the trading window runs from November 29 to December 2, then the auction list is locked before December 7.
