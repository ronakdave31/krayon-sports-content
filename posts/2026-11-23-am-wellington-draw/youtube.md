**Title**
India 720 vs New Zealand Still a Draw | Mitchell and Phillips Save Wellington Test #Shorts

**Description**
The first Test between New Zealand and India in Wellington ended in a draw. India had declared on 720 for 9, but New Zealand batted through the final day and finished on 239 for 5 in 120 overs.

Daryl Mitchell faced 186 balls for 49 not out and Glenn Phillips faced 126 balls for 26 not out. India took three wickets on day 5 (Conway, Ravindra and Blundell) but none after Blundell fell in the 77th over.

The two-Test series stays level. The second Test starts in Christchurch on Friday, 27 November.
