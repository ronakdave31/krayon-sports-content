**Title**
Antonelli fastest in Las Vegas GP qualifying but Verstappen gets pole #Shorts

**Description**
Kimi Antonelli, already the 2026 Formula 1 world champion, set the fastest lap in Las Vegas Grand Prix qualifying with a 1:33.893. Max Verstappen was 0.258s slower but starts on pole.

Why: Antonelli takes a five-place grid penalty for new power unit parts, so he starts sixth. Oscar Piastri and Carlos Sainz also have 10-place power unit penalties.

What's next: the title is settled but second place in the standings is not. Verstappen has 279 points to George Russell's 262 and they start together on the front row.
