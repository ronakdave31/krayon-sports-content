**Title**
Portugal 1-4 Denmark: first game without Cristiano Ronaldo | Nations League #Shorts

**Description**
Portugal played their first match since Cristiano Ronaldo retired from international football and lost 4-1 to Denmark in Braga in the UEFA Nations League. Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg scored a penalty, Rasmus Hojlund scored twice and Joachim Andersen headed in a fourth before Bernardo Silva pulled one back in stoppage time.

The night was meant to honour Ronaldo. Every Portugal player and mascot wore a Ronaldo 7 shirt before kick-off and fans held up a "thank you, captain" banner. Ronaldo leaves with 146 goals in 234 games for Portugal.

Portugal are already in the quarter-finals. They play Wales in Cardiff on November 17 while Denmark host Norway the same night.
