**Title:** India beat China 3-1 in Women's Asian Champions Trophy final for record fourth title #Shorts

**Description:**
India beat China 3-1 in the Women's Asian Champions Trophy final in Bhopal. China led 1-0 at half-time through Chen Yujun's 25th-minute goal.

India turned it around with three goals in six minutes of the third quarter: Deepika from a penalty corner (38'), Ishika (42') and Deepika from another penalty corner (43'). Deepika finishes the tournament as top scorer with 12 goals.

It is India's third title in a row (2023, 2024, 2026) and a record fourth overall. India also beat China 6-2 in the pool stage earlier this week.
