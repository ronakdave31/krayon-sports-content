**Title**
India 7-0 Thailand: Six Set-Piece Goals | Women's Asian Champions Trophy Hockey #Shorts

**Description**
Defending champions India beat Thailand 7-0 in Bhopal to reach the semi-finals of the Women's Asian Champions Trophy with two group games to spare.

Six of the seven goals came from set pieces. Deepika scored a hat-trick of penalty corner goals, Navneet Kaur converted two penalty strokes and Sunelita Toppo added another penalty corner. Mumtaz Khan scored the only goal from open play.

India have won all three games and top the table on goal difference ahead of Japan. They play China next on Tuesday, November 24.
