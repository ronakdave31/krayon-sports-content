**Title:** IPL 2027 auction register: 1,220 players for only 66 contracts #Shorts

**Description:**
The IPL 2027 auction register is out. 1,220 players have signed up: 939 Indians and 281 overseas players. The ten teams have only 66 squad spots to fill at the auction in Goa on December 16.

The biggest group is 798 uncapped Indians who have never played an IPL game. Only 14 capped India players are on the list. Overseas, 281 players are chasing 25 spots. Ben Stokes and David Warner are in the top Rs 2 crore base-price bracket.

The final auction list and player sets come out on December 4. The teams have Rs 295.60 crore left between them.
