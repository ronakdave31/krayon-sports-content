**Title:** NZ vs India 2nd Test Day 2: Akash Deep takes 5 as New Zealand are all out for 356 #Shorts

**Description:**
Akash Deep took 5 for 99 on day 2 of the second Test at Hagley Oval as New Zealand were bowled out for 356. Tom Blundell top-scored with 89 from 211 balls before debutant Gurnoor Brar had him caught at fine leg.

Jasprit Bumrah bowled 26 overs for 49 runs without taking a wicket. Brar and Anshul Kamboj took two wickets each and Washington Sundar took one.

India's reply started badly. Yashasvi Jaiswal fell for a duck in the first over and Kyle Jamieson removed Devdutt Padikkal and Shubman Gill. India were 59 for 3 at tea, 297 behind. Wellington was a draw, so this Test decides the series.
