**Title**
India 720/9 vs New Zealand: Highest Test Total Ever in NZ | Pant, Rahul, Gill #Shorts

**Description**
India declared on 720 for 9 on day 4 of the first Test against New Zealand in Wellington. It is the highest Test total ever made in New Zealand, passing New Zealand's 715 for 6 against Bangladesh in 2019.

Nobody failed. KL Rahul made 136, Rishabh Pant 128, Shubman Gill 98, Yashasvi Jaiswal 85, Devdutt Padikkal 80 and Dhruv Jurel 78. It is India's third-highest Test total ever and gave them a first-innings lead of 352.

New Zealand now have to bat to save the Test, with Tom Latham already out to Jasprit Bumrah.
