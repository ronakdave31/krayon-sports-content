NZ vs India 1st Test Day 2: Jaiswal 85 Rahul 52* | India 159 for 1 in Wellington #Shorts

**Description**
New Zealand were bowled out for 368 at the Basin Reserve in Wellington. India's openers replied with a 130 stand before Matt Henry had Yashasvi Jaiswal caught behind for 85. India closed day 2 on 159 for 1.

The stand worked because the openers played two different roles. Jaiswal made 85 off 89 balls with 16 fours. KL Rahul made 52 not out off 116 balls and kept his end safe.

Rahul and Devdutt Padikkal resume on day 3 with India 209 runs behind and nine wickets in hand.
