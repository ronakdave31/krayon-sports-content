**Title**
Rakshitha Ramraj, 19, beats Olympic champion Chen Yu Fei at Hong Kong Open #Shorts

**Description**
Rakshitha Ramraj beat Tokyo 2020 Olympic champion Chen Yu Fei 21-18, 24-22 at the Hong Kong Open Super 500 on Thursday to reach the quarter-finals. Chen is world No. 4. A day earlier the 19-year-old beat world No. 20 Mia Blichfeldt 21-18, 21-18.

Also in Hong Kong: Kidambi Srikanth beat Koki Watanabe 21-16, 16-21, 21-9. Watanabe was Lakshya Sen's nearest rival in the Race to Finals (73,620 points to 74,180) so Lakshya is through to the BWF World Tour Finals.

What's next: Rakshitha plays Pornpawee Chochuwong, Lakshya plays Loh Kean Yew and Satwik and Chirag play Hu Ke Yuan and Lin Xiang Yi in the quarter-finals. The World Tour Finals are in Hangzhou from December 9 to 13.
