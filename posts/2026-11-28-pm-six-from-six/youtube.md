**Title:** India vs China Women's Asian Champions Trophy final: six wins from six, one to go #Shorts

**Description:**
India's women play China in the Women's Asian Champions Trophy hockey final in Bhopal tonight at 7 pm IST. They have won all six games so far and a win would be a third straight title.

When the two sides met on November 24, India were 3-0 up inside 14 minutes through a Deepika hat-trick and won 6-2. Dainik Jagran reports Deepika has 10 goals at the tournament with nine from penalty corners.

China reached the final by beating Japan 1-0 with a 60th-minute goal. Former Olympian Sameer Dad rates India's chances at 60:40.
