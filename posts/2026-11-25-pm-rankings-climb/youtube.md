**Title:** ICC Test rankings: Pant, Gill and Jaiswal in the top 10 after India vs New Zealand #Shorts

**Description:**
The first Test between New Zealand and India in Wellington ended in a draw. India's batters still moved up the new ICC Test rankings after declaring on 720/9.

Rishabh Pant is up to 6th after his 128. Shubman Gill is 7th after 98 and Yashasvi Jaiswal jumps five places to 9th. KL Rahul's 136 lifts him to 25th and Devdutt Padikkal gains 15 places. Jasprit Bumrah's five wickets take him to joint second among Test bowlers.

The deciding second Test starts at Hagley Oval in Christchurch on November 26.
