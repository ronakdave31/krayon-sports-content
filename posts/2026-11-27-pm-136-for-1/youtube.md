**Title:** NZ vs India 2nd Test Day 1: From 110 for 4 to 246 for 5 in Christchurch #Shorts

**Description:**
India had New Zealand at 110 for 4 on day 1 of the second Test at Hagley Oval. Akash Deep and Anshul Kamboj took three wickets without a run being added. New Zealand still finished the day on 246 for 5.

Tom Blundell rebuilt the innings. He added 74 with Daryl Mitchell and an unbeaten 62 with Glenn Phillips. New Zealand scored 115 runs in the final session for the loss of one wicket. Jasprit Bumrah went wicketless in 19 overs and debutant Gurnoor Brar took 1 for 52.

Wellington was a draw, so this Test decides the series. Blundell (53*) and Phillips (36*) resume on day 2.
