**Title**
IPL 2027 Auction Purses: Who Can Break Rishabh Pant's Rs 27 Crore Record? #Shorts

**Description**
The IPL 2027 mini-auction is in Goa on December 16. After retentions and trades the ten teams have Rs 297.95 crore left between them and 66 squad spots to fill. LSG have the most at Rs 47.45 crore. GT have the least at Rs 8.75 crore.

Rishabh Pant's Rs 27 crore from the 2025 auction is the record. Six teams have more than that to spend: LSG, KKR, SRH, DC, MI and CSK. RR have Rs 27.05 crore with six spots to fill. At a mini-auction an overseas player is paid at most Rs 18 crore and anything bid above that goes to the BCCI, so an Indian player is the one who keeps a record bid in full.

Who goes past Rs 27 crore in Goa? Tell us in the comments.
