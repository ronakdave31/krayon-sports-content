**Title**
NZ vs India 1st Test Day 1: 70 for 5 then Nicholls hundred | Bumrah 3 wickets #Shorts

**Description**
India won the toss and bowled first at the Basin Reserve in Wellington. Jasprit Bumrah bowled Tom Latham in the first over and took three wickets as New Zealand slipped to 70 for 5 in the 29th over.

Then India could not take another wicket. Henry Nicholls and Glenn Phillips put on an unbroken stand of more than 100 for the 6th wicket and Nicholls reached his hundred in the final session. India missed a review on a Rachin Ravindra edge and lost one against Phillips.

India's bowlers need early wickets on day 2 before Shubman Gill's batters get their turn. The 2nd Test starts in Christchurch on November 27.
