**Title:** Sporting 2-2 Man United: 3 penalties, 2 goals ruled out, still no Pochettino win #Shorts

**Description:**
Sporting and Manchester United drew 2-2 in the Champions League in Lisbon. Luis Suárez scored twice for Sporting and Bruno Fernandes scored twice for United, one of them a penalty.

Senne Lammens saved one penalty but gave away another and then missed the cross for Sporting's late equaliser. United had two goals ruled out, the second by VAR for a foul on the keeper, and Youri Tielemans was sent off in stoppage time.

Pochettino is still waiting for his first win. United have conceded two or more in seven straight games, per Opta Analyst, and sit 19th in the league phase. Brentford are next on Saturday.
