**Title:** Mumbai Indians cryptic post: is Rohit Sharma back as MI captain for IPL 2027? #Shorts

**Description:**
Mumbai Indians posted a picture on X on Wednesday with no caption: just a hand holding the MI cap. Fans read it one way. They think Rohit Sharma is coming back as captain.

MI need a new captain for IPL 2027. Hardik Pandya, who led them from 2024, has been traded to Punjab Kings with Arshdeep Singh moving to Mumbai. Rohit led MI for 11 seasons and won five titles: 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2020.

MI have not confirmed anything yet. Suryakumar Yadav, Jasprit Bumrah and Tilak Varma have also been named in reports as options.
