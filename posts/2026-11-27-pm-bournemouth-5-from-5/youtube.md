**Title:** Celta 1-2 Bournemouth: 10 men, first season in Europe, 5 wins from 5 #Shorts

**Description:**
Bournemouth beat Celta Vigo 2-1 in the Europa League with 10 men. Iago Aspas put Celta ahead and Bafode Diakite was sent off after a VAR review. Alvaro Rodriguez headed the equaliser early in the second half and substitute Rayan won it on 78 minutes.

In their first season in Europe, Bournemouth have won all five games. Sky Sports and beIN Sports say no English club had done that before in major European competition. It is 10 straight wins in all competitions and they top the league phase on 15 points.

Celta had 1.83 xG to Bournemouth's 0.63, per beIN Sports. Fulham away on Sunday is next, with a club-record sixth straight Premier League win on the line.
