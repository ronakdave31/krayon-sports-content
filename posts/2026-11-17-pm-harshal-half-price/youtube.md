**Title:** Harshal Patel traded to Gujarat Titans at half price: SRH to GT for Rs 4 crore #Shorts

**Description:**
Sunrisers Hyderabad have traded Harshal Patel to Gujarat Titans in an all-cash deal at a revised fee of Rs 4 crore. SRH paid Rs 8 crore for him at the IPL 2025 mega auction.

Why the price halved: he took 16 wickets in 13 matches in 2025, then went wicketless in 5 matches in IPL 2026 at 10.82 runs an over. He still has 151 IPL wickets and two purple caps (2021 with RCB, 2024 with PBKS). GT will be his fifth IPL team.

The trade came on IPL 2027 retention deadline day. All 10 teams name their retained and released players today and the mini-auction is in Goa on December 16.
